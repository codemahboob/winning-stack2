Return & Refund Policy

Last updated — July 2026

The Winning Products Bundle is a digital product delivered instantly by email after purchase. Because the content is downloadable and viewable immediately, please read this policy carefully before buying.

As the bundle is delivered digitally and instantly, all sales are final once the file has been accessed or downloaded. We're unable to offer refunds simply on the basis of change of mind.

When a refund applies

  • You were charged but never received the download link or access email within 24 hours.
  • The file you received is corrupted, incomplete, or does not match what was described on this page.
  • You were charged more than once for the same order due to a technical error.

When a refund does not apply

  • You've already downloaded or opened the bundle and simply changed your mind.
  • Results from running the products or ad creative vary — performance depends on your execution, market, and ad account, and isn't guaranteed.
  • The request is made more than 7 days after purchase.

How to request a refund

Email us at support@digiecom.example with your order ID (from your Razorpay payment confirmation) and the reason for your request. We aim to respond within 2 business days and, where approved, process refunds back to your original payment method within 5–7 business days.

Contact

Questions about an order or this policy — reach out any time at support@digiecom.example.
